Cyber Security Tips

How to Stay Safe Online

Introduction

With the growth of internet usage, protecting personal information online has become very important. Cyber security helps keep your data, accounts, and devices safe.

1. Use Strong Passwords

Create unique passwords with a combination of letters, numbers, and symbols. Avoid using the same password for multiple accounts.

2. Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication adds an extra layer of security to your online accounts.

3. Keep Software Updated

Always update your operating system and apps to get the latest security improvements.

4. Avoid Suspicious Links

Do not open unknown links or download files from untrusted sources.

5. Protect Personal Information

Avoid sharing sensitive information online and check privacy settings on social media accounts.

6. Use Secure Networks

Be careful when using public Wi-Fi networks and avoid accessing important accounts on unsafe connections.

Conclusion

Following basic cyber security practices can help protect you from online threats and keep your digital life safer.
