Apple iPhone 16 Pro Max

The Apple iPhone 16 Pro Max is Apple's premium flagship smartphone featuring a large 6.9-inch Super Retina XDR OLED display, powerful A18 Pro chip, advanced triple-camera system and premium titanium design.

Specifications

  • Display: 6.9-inch Super Retina XDR OLED, 120Hz
  • Resolution: 2868 × 1320 Pixels
  • Processor: Apple A18 Pro Chip
  • RAM: 8GB
  • Storage: 256GB / 512GB / 1TB
  • Rear Camera: 48MP + 48MP Ultra Wide + 12MP Telephoto (5x Zoom)
  • Front Camera: 12MP TrueDepth
  • Battery: Long-lasting Battery with Fast Charging
  • Operating System: iOS 18
  • Security: Face ID
  • Network: 5G
  • Water Resistance: IP68
  • Colors: Black Titanium, White Titanium, Natural Titanium, Desert Titanium

Key Features

  • Premium Titanium Body
  • Dynamic Island
  • Always-On Display
  • 120Hz ProMotion Display
  • Apple Intelligence Support
  • Action Button
  • Camera Control Button
  • Professional 4K Video Recording
  • USB-C Port
