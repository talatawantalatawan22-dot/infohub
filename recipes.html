🍗 Chicken Biryani

Chicken Biryani is a delicious and popular rice dish made with chicken, rice, spices, yogurt, and fresh herbs.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500g chicken
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Biryani spices
  • Fresh coriander and mint
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Wash and soak the rice for 20 minutes.
  2. Fry sliced onions until golden brown.
  3. Add chicken, tomatoes, yogurt and spices.
  4. Cook until the chicken becomes tender.
  5. Boil the rice until almost cooked.
  6. Layer the rice and chicken mixture in a pot.
  7. Cover and cook on low heat for 15–20 minutes.
  8. Mix gently and serve hot.

🥔 Aloo Paratha

Aloo Paratha is a simple and tasty Pakistani and Indian flatbread filled with seasoned mashed potatoes.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups wheat flour
  • 3 boiled potatoes
  • 1 small onion
  • Green chilies
  • Fresh coriander
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • Salt to taste
  • Cooking oil or ghee

Method

  1. Prepare a soft dough using wheat flour and water.
  2. Mash the boiled potatoes.
  3. Add onion, green chilies, coriander and spices.
  4. Roll the dough and place the potato filling inside.
  5. Close and roll it into a round paratha.
  6. Cook on a hot pan using oil or ghee.
  7. Cook both sides until golden and serve hot.

🍝 Creamy Chicken Pasta

Creamy Chicken Pasta is an easy and delicious meal made with chicken, pasta, cream and simple spices.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups pasta
  • 250g chicken
  • 1 cup cooking cream
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese

Method

  1. Boil the pasta until tender and drain it.
  2. Heat butter and cook the garlic.
  3. Add chicken and cook until fully done.
  4. Add cream, black pepper and salt.
  5. Add the cooked pasta and mix well.
  6. Add cheese and cook for a few minutes.
  7. Serve hot.

🍰 Simple Chocolate Cake

A simple homemade chocolate cake that is easy to prepare and perfect for family tea time or special occasions.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla essence

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix flour, cocoa powder, sugar and baking powder.
  3. Add eggs, milk, oil and vanilla essence.
  4. Mix until the batter becomes smooth.
  5. Pour the batter into a greased cake pan.
  6. Bake for about 25–30 minutes.
  7. Let the cake cool before serving.
